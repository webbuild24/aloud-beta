What you're testing

One loop: hear it, check it, keep it.

Listen

  • Reads PDF, Word, PowerPoint, EPUB, text and Markdown
  • Natural on-device voices, adjustable speed and pitch
  • Background playback with lock-screen and headphone controls
  • Skips running headers and page numbers; narrates tables row by row
  • Tap any line to start there; swipe to turn pages

Understand

  • Quiz me on the passage you just heard — multiple choice and short answer
  • Short summaries before you commit to a section

  • Offline quizzes built on-device — no account, no connection

Remember

  • Study plans: timed sessions, a quiz after each, a final exam
  • A review deck that brings back the questions you missed, on schedule
  • Streaks, a progress dashboard, highlights, notes and search
  • One-tap study-guide export
How the beta works

Three steps.

Install TestFlight

Get Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store if you don't have it yet.

Open the invite

Tap the join link on your iPhone or iPad, then “Install”. Updates arrive automatically.

Send feedback from inside the app

Take a screenshot in TestFlight and add a note, or press-and-hold with two fingers. That's the fastest way to reach us.

Good to know

It's an early build.

The app icon is a placeholder. A final one is coming before public release.

Everything core works with no account and no connection — listening, offline quizzes, the review deck, your library.

Your documents stay on your device. They only leave it if you choose a cloud AI feature (richer quizzes and summaries).

Some AI features need a connection or an Aloud Pro subscription; the free, on-device versions are always available.

Things will change. Voices, quiz quality, and table reading are active areas — tell us where they fall short.

No ads, no tracking.

Support

Questions or a bug?

The quickest route is TestFlight's built-in feedback: screenshot inside the app, add a note, send. It reaches us with your device and build details attached.

For anything else, email you@example.com. We usually reply within two days.
